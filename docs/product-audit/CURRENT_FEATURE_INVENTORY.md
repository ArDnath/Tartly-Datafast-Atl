## 10. Bottom line

Genuinely implemented and deep: ingestion, session/report SQL, funnels/journeys/retention/UTM,
payment webhooks + first/last-touch matching, auth/workspace security, platform billing state
machine, exports/imports, share dashboards, admin.

Not delivered despite being promised: revenue-by-channel UI, MRR-by-channel UI, advanced
attribution, and enforcement of most Pro-only entitlements. Session replay is now switchable but
its recorder is documented outside onboarding.
