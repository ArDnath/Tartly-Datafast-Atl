# Positioning and messaging

## One sentence

**RevenueLens shows indie SaaS founders which traffic sources actually produce
paying customers, for $7.99/month.**

## Who it is for

- Indie hackers and solo founders shipping their first paid product.
- Micro-SaaS and AI SaaS founders who already have traffic and a payment
  provider.
- Small dev teams and tiny agencies that need one shared revenue view without
  an enterprise contract.

Not for: enterprises needing SSO, custom data residency, a 99.99% SLA, or a
dedicated success manager. Those requirements belong to a different product.

## The problem

Founders can see traffic in one tool and revenue in Stripe/Dodo/Lemon Squeezy/
Polar/Yolfi in another. They cannot answer the only question that matters:
*which channel, campaign, page, or journey produced the money?* Existing
revenue-attribution tools are either broad product-analytics suites or priced
for teams with a marketing budget.

## The promise

1. Install one script.
2. Connect the payment provider you already use.
3. See revenue by source, campaign, landing page, and journey.
4. Pay a price a solo founder can keep on a card for years.

## Proof points (only what the product does)

- First-touch and last-touch attribution across organic, paid, referral,
  direct, recurring, and unattributed journeys.
- Visitors, sessions, pageviews, bounce, duration, geography, device, browser,
  OS, screen, referrers, landing/exit pages, UTM, goals, funnels, retention,
  cohorts, realtime, and session replay (Pro).
- Stripe, Dodo Payments, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Yolfi, and a manual payment API.
- Two plans: Starter $7.99/month (1 website) and Pro $14.99/month (up to 20).
- No free tier. No invented invoices. No fake customer counts.

| Secondary CTA | See the live demo |
| Objection handler | No free tier, but no annual lock-in and no per-seat tax. Cancel at the end of the paid period. |
| Trust | MIT-licensed foundation, first-touch and last-touch attribution, your data stays in your workspace. |

## Words to avoid

- "AI-powered" unless a shipped feature actually uses AI. Attribution is
  deterministic.
- "Unlimited" for anything the plan limits.
- "GDPR compliant" as a blanket claim. Say what the product does (cookieless
  mode where supported, consent hooks, export/deletion, DPA draft) and let
  counsel review.
- Competitor prices without a source and date.
- Testimonials or customer counts that do not exist yet.

## Pricing narrative

Two plans, deliberately narrow:

- **Starter $7.99** — one website, the full revenue loop, for a founder proving
  the first channel.
- **Pro $14.99** — up to 20 websites, replay, longer retention, for a small team
  or agency.

The price is below Plausible Starter ($9), Fathom's entry plan ($15), Simple
Analytics ($20), and Umami Pro ($20) as displayed on 2026-11-06. Limits differ;
compare the plan table, not the headline only. DataFast's public price could not
be verified without JavaScript rendering on 2026-11-06, so do not quote it.
